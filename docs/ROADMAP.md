# What is current, and what is next?

## Explore the current product

The guide covers profile onboarding and analysis, conversational outfit discovery and advice, garment-photo and wardrobe entry points, product inspection and retailer links, virtual try-on where available, look history and saving, product-level wishlisting, sharing, profile editing, the retailer directory, feature requests, and account/help surfaces.

Feature availability can depend on your session, market, usable images, and the products the app can retrieve. Use the app’s displayed state when a particular action is unavailable.

## In development and verification

| Direction | What it aims to improve |
| --- | --- |
| Multi-day capsule planning | Plan outfits across days and activities with a coherent packing list and purchase subtotal. |
| Broader wardrobe combinations | Work more reliably with multiple garment photos, saved pieces, and owned-versus-new constraints. |
| More precise follow-ups | Preserve the intended outfit, retained items, and selected product variants across turns. |
| Practical shopping constraints | Improve evidence handling for size, stock, materials, delivery needs, and other concrete requirements. |
| End-to-end quality checks | Verify full customer journeys and product evidence across the supported markets. |

These are ongoing work, not promises that every corresponding request is already fulfilled. This page makes no release-date commitment.

## Current scope

- Shopping markets: **India and the United States**.
- Shopping focus: supported clothing. Footwear styling in generated imagery does not imply shoe search, shoe stock, or a purchasable shoe recommendation.
- Intimate-apparel shopping is outside the current scope.
- Retailer checkout, payment, shipping, and returns happen with the retailer.
- AI visualization does not certify physical fit or delivery.
