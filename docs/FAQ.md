# Questions before your first look

<details open>
<summary><strong>How do I use Vibe?</strong></summary>

[Open Vibe](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/), verify your mobile number, complete your profile and photos, and wait for analysis. Then describe what you want to wear. [Follow the steps →](GETTING_STARTED.md)

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Do I need a code or invitation?</strong></summary>

No invitation code is required. You do need the one-time sign-in code sent to your mobile number. Vibe is for adults aged 18 and above.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which countries and currencies are supported?</strong></summary>

Shopping is supported in India with INR and the United States with USD. Vibe detects the market from your network location. It does not provide a country picker to override this. Measurement units can be changed separately in your profile.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why do I need to add photos and measurements?</strong></summary>

The current application requires completed profile inputs, a headshot, a full-body photo, and successful analysis before recommendations. The profile helps provide styling context; it is not a measurement of how a particular retailer’s garment will physically fit. [Read about photo processing →](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/privacy.html)

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I use something I already own?</strong></summary>

Yes. Add a garment to [Wardrobe](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/wardrobe.html), then select it in Chat, or attach a garment photo directly to your request. Explain which item you own and what you want to pair with it. [Try an example →](TRY_VIBE.md#style-something-you-own)

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I ask for one garment or advice without shopping?</strong></summary>

You can ask for a supported clothing piece instead of a complete outfit. For a style question, say that you want advice and no products. [See example prompts →](TRY_VIBE.md)

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I buy inside Vibe?</strong></summary>

Product actions take you to independent retailers. Purchases, payment, delivery, and returns are handled by the retailer. Confirm the final product and offer on its site.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why is virtual try-on unavailable on a particular look?</strong></summary>

It depends on feature availability, completed profile/photo requirements, and usable garment images. A look without suitable images may not offer the action. If an enabled action repeatedly fails, [contact Support](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/support.html).

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Where are my saved outfits and previews?</strong></summary>

Open [Looks](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/looks.html) and use the saved-look or virtual-try-on filter. Sign in with the same mobile number to access your saved profile.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can Vibe plan my whole trip or buy shoes?</strong></summary>

Multi-day capsule planning is in development. The shopping focus is supported clothing. Generated footwear styling is separate from shoe catalog search and purchase recommendations. Intimate-apparel shopping is outside the current scope. [See current boundaries and next work →](ROADMAP.md)

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What if I lose access to my mobile number?</strong></summary>

The current account system cannot transfer or recover a profile under a different number. Read the [account-access support guidance](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/support.html) before taking action.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How can I manage or delete my data?</strong></summary>

Use the profile menu’s **Help & Privacy** area and the app’s data controls. Read [Privacy](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/privacy.html) for export, consent, retention, and deletion information. Signing out does not delete the account.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Are the screenshots and video on GitHub available yet?</strong></summary>

They are clearly labeled placeholders. You can use the real application now or follow the [written demo walkthrough](DEMO.md). The placeholders will be replaced with real captures of a stable product version.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Where can I report a problem or request a feature?</strong></summary>

Use [Support](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/support.html) for account, retailer, or privacy issues. Use the [feature-request board](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/feature-requests.html) to submit and vote for ideas.

</details>
