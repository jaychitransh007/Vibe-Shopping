# What would you like to try?

Choose a prompt, use GitHub’s copy control on its code block, and paste it into [Vibe Chat ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/) after onboarding. These are starting prompts, not fixed demo responses. Suggestions depend on your profile, market, request, and available products.

## Dress for an occasion

```text
I have a casual dinner on Saturday.
Find me a relaxed outfit under ₹8,000 in black or earth tones.
```

Add the formality, setting, and any clothing requirements that matter. Use your actual currency and budget for the detected shopping market.

[Try it in Vibe ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/)

## Style something you own

Attach a garment photo in Chat, or choose an item from your saved wardrobe. Then say:

```text
I already own this linen shirt.
Help me build a casual dinner outfit around it.
Only suggest new pieces for the rest of the look.
```

[Add a wardrobe item ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/wardrobe.html) · [Open Chat ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/)

## Find a single piece

```text
Show me casual cotton shirts under ₹2,500.
I only want a shirt, not a complete outfit.
```

Name the garment and preferences clearly. The current shopping focus is supported clothing; footwear styling is distinct from buying shoes.

[Try it in Vibe ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/)

## Refine a look

After receiving outfit suggestions, refer to the one you mean:

```text
In outfit 2, keep the shirt and change the trousers.
Make the look feel less formal and keep my original budget.
```

If you want a fresh direction, say what should change and what should stay. Precise references help Vibe interpret the follow-up.

[Continue in Vibe ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/)

## Understand a recommendation

```text
Why did you recommend the second outfit?
Explain how its colors and silhouette relate to my request.
```

Read the product details alongside the explanation. A styling explanation is different from a retailer’s confirmed size or fit measurements.

[Ask Vibe ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/)

## Ask for style advice

```text
What is the difference between smart casual and business casual?
Give me styling advice without showing products.
```

You can explore a style direction before deciding whether to shop.

[Ask Vibe ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/)

## Visualize and keep a look

This part uses the outfit card rather than a prompt:

1. Open an eligible recommendation.
2. Select **Virtual try-on** when the action is available.
3. Save a look you like.
4. Revisit it in **Looks history** and use **Share look** after a try-on is available.

[Open Looks ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/looks.html) · [Read the try-on and sharing guide](FEATURES.md#virtual-try-on)
