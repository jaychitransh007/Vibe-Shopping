# Your first conversation with Vibe

## 1. Open Vibe

[Start here ↗](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/)

Vibe supports shopping in **India and the United States**. It detects the shopping market from your network location and uses INR or USD accordingly. You must be **18 or older**. There is no invitation code.

## 2. Sign in

Enter your mobile number and verify the one-time code. Use the same number when returning to your saved profile. Sign-in is required before profile onboarding and styling.

## 3. Complete your style profile

Follow the onboarding cards for your profile details, date of birth, measurements, a headshot, and a full-body photo. Use clear photos you are entitled to upload. The headshot should show your face clearly; the full-body image should show you from head to toe in good light.

Wait for the profile analysis to complete. If a photo needs replacing or analysis fails, follow the app’s retry instructions. Successful, current analysis is required to unlock recommendations.

After onboarding, [Profile](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/profile.html) lets you review and update these details. A change to the underlying inputs can require a fresh analysis.

## 4. Describe what you need

Give Vibe the occasion, your preferred direction, and a budget. You can name a garment, a color, or something you want to avoid.

```text
Find me a relaxed dinner outfit under ₹8,000.
I like black and earth tones. Nothing too formal.
```

For a US shopping session, use your own budget in USD instead. [More starting points →](TRY_VIBE.md)

## 5. Explore the result

Browse the outfit carousel, open an individual garment, read the explanation, and inspect the retailer details. Use the card’s available actions to save the look, shortlist a piece, generate a try-on, or open the retailer.

You can follow up with a specific change:

```text
In outfit 2, keep the shirt and change the trousers.
Keep the whole look within my original budget.
```

## 6. Come back to what you like

- [Looks](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/looks.html): history, saved looks, theme filters, and generated try-ons.
- [Wardrobe](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/wardrobe.html): upload and view clothes you own.
- [Profile](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/profile.html): details, photos, and style analysis.

Account-page links use your current session. If they ask you to sign in, return to [Chat](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/) and complete the sign-in flow first.

## Need a hand?

[Read the FAQ](FAQ.md) or [open support](https://vibe-consumer-web.vercel.app/support.html). For a product issue, include the affected product title, retailer domain, and a short description. Keep sign-in codes and personal account details out of public GitHub issues.
